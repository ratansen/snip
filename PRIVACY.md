# Snip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Snip does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or browsing data.

## What Snip stores

The only data Snip stores is the list of rules you create (URL prefix, id pattern, class pattern, and whether each rule is enabled). These rules are saved with Chrome's `chrome.storage.sync` API, which keeps them in your browser and, if you have Chrome sync turned on, syncs them across your own signed-in Chrome browsers through your Google account. The developer has no access to this data.

## Page content

Snip's content script runs on the pages you visit so that it can remove the elements your rules describe. It reads element ids and class names locally in your browser only to decide what to remove. Nothing from the page is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

## Network access

Snip makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and includes no third-party code.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/ratansen/snip/issues.
